Luke Snow

I'm a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Yale University, where I am fortunate to be hosted by Professor Boris Landa. My research interests are broadly in statistical signal processing, stochastic analysis and its application to machine learning, and algorithmic game theory. See my research page for more details.

In September of 2026 I obtained my Ph.D. in electrical engineering from Cornell University, where I was advised by Professor Vikram Krishnamurthy within the Cornell Statistical Signal Processing Lab.

I have professional research experience through internships with Basis Research Institute (2026), the U.S. Army Research Laboratory (2022), and the MIT Lincoln Laboratory (2021).
